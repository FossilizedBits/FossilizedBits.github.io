Fossilized Bits was founded in 2014 with one idea: make simple tasks simpler. We build for niche markets, starting with the drilling floor, where the same calculations get worked by hand or in a spreadsheet tour after tour.

Each app does one job, works offline and shows its math, so you can check the answer against your own numbers.
